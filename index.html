Our Story

A Retreat by the Carolina Coast

Tucked away in the heart of Wilmington, The Cove Hair Studio is more than a salon — it's a retreat. Named for the quiet beauty of the Carolina coast, our studio was born from a shared love of craft, community, and making every client feel genuinely seen.

We are Tara and Kayla, two stylists who believe that great hair is deeply personal. Whether you're looking for a bold transformation or a subtle refresh, we take the time to listen, consult, and create looks that feel authentically you.

At The Cove, you'll find a calm, welcoming atmosphere where artistry and connection come together. Come as you are — leave feeling like the best version of yourself.
